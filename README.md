# RAEE-Teal-Browser

Android glass-style browser by RAEE-INDUSTRIES, featuring Xenphia.

## Download version 0.8 preview

- [GitHub preview release](https://github.com/wsylla26-create/RAEE-Teal-Browser/releases/tag/v0.8.0-preview)
- [Download APK from RAEE-INDUSTRIES on Shopify](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0606/5972/8433/files/RAEE-Teal-Browser-test_3d2a34fb-a674-4b15-a27d-277a45e1dc1e.apk?v=1793668335)
- [Visit RAEE-INDUSTRIES](https://raee-industries.me/)

Download `RAEE-Teal-Browser-test.apk` on an Android 8.0+ phone, open the file, and follow Android's installation prompts. The app appears as **Teal Browser Preview**. This is a debug-signed preview for testing, not a Google Play release.

## Included

- Xenphia says hello when the browser opens; optional greeting switch in the voice menu.
- English, Spanish, French and Italian phone speech; RAEE is pronounced “Ray.”
- Phone voice commands and search, a blinking home portrait, and teal/green/pink glass fades with pause.
- Compact labeled navigation, additional features in a dropdown, portrait and landscape layouts.
- HTTPS WebView browsing, tabs, bookmarks, restored tab addresses, and text-size controls.

General AI conversation and precise lip-sync are not connected in this browser build. Voice availability depends on installed phone voices and recognition services. On-device acceptance testing remains required.
